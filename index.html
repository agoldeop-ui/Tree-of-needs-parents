Facilitator Guide

Phase 1: The Gate

"Hi [Name]. Thanks for making the time today. I know with a 2-year-old and two older kids, your time is basically a luxury right now.

Before we start, I want to remind you that this is a guided reflection, not therapy or diagnosis. You can skip any question, change your mind, or stop at any time. If anything feels too heavy, we pause.

Are you still happy to take part today? And are you comfortable with me recording this session?"

Phase 2: Baseline

1. "Why do you want to do this today?"
2. "Before we start, what do you think your biggest need is right now?"
3. "If this session is useful, what would be different for you afterward—not in your whole life, but in one small, observable way?"

Phase 3 & 4: The Tree

"The Tree is not a ladder. You don't have to fix the bottom first. Today's tree only needs to be honest."

"Where do you feel strain, growth, protection, or possibility today?"

Phase 7: The Flinch

"There is a harder section about needs that can look ugly from the outside: rage, the need to hide, the need to control everything. These are not bad needs. They are often protections. Do you want to go there?"

Phase 8: The Anchor

"This tree is not a project to perfect. What would 'enough' look like for you this season—not perfect, not permanently fixed, but enough?"

Phase 10: The Return

"At the start, you said your biggest need was [Read her baseline answer]. What do you say now?"
